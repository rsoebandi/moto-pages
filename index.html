Moto

Moto is a private, single-user accounting assistant built and used by its owner, Ivan Soebandi, for his own business bookkeeping.

It runs on the owner's own computer. It reads bank statement PDFs and spreadsheets that the owner places in his own Google Drive folders, prepares journal files in another folder of the same Drive, and reports back to the owner through a private Telegram chat.

Moto is not offered to the public and has no other users. Nobody except the owner signs in.
