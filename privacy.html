Moto - Privacy Policy

Last updated: 16 October 2026

Moto is a private tool used only by its owner, Ivan Soebandi. It has no other users.

What Moto accesses

When the owner signs in with Google, Moto can read and create files in the owner's own Google Drive and Google Sheets: bank statement files and spreadsheets the owner places in his Drive, and journal files that Moto prepares for him.

How the data is used

The data is used only to prepare the owner's accounting journals. It is processed on the owner's own computer. It is not sold, shared, published, or used for advertising, and it is not used to train any model.

Storage

The Google sign-in token is stored only on the owner's computer. Moto keeps no copy of Google data on any server of its own.

Deleting access

The owner can revoke Moto's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
